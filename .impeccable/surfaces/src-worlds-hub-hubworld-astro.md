# Hub surface (/, /en/, 404)

Scope: the CyberTech team hub at `/` (ru) and `/en/` (en), plus the bilingual 404 in the same world. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Mac owners (ru/en) arriving from GitHub, shared links or search. Job: see every utility at a glance, understand each in one line, trust the shared promises, and open one product's page (download lives on product pages). Proof: real app icons, real release data from `releases.json` (version, date, macOS floor, licence). Constraints: no invented claims (no founding year, no slogans, no counts); product identities stay the products'; data-driven so a 4th product adds a band; no JS needed; shell contract `--shell-*` under `:root[data-world="hub"]`.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/hub-comp-3.png` (composition 3, monumental catalogue numbers). Decision comp `.impeccable/mocks/decision/model-pick.png` is option one, not approved. Do not build the comp's invented copy ("Est. 2026", footer slogan); the four promises appear once as one strip, not repeated per band.

Memorable moment: the catalogue numbers at monumental scale, the newest release burning fluorescent orange.

## Direction contract

THESIS: CyberTech team as an independent label; the hub is its catalogue and every utility a numbered release, CT 01, CT 02, CT 03. It refuses the category default: a hero headline over a row of equal app cards.

OWN-WORLD: grey sleeve-board with visible fibre as the ground; black ultra-condensed grotesk for catalogue numbers and names; a typewriter-mono for pressing data; fluorescent orange only for the newest release and its arrow; one silver-foil label mark; app icons blind-embossed into the board; heavy black rules between bands.

STORY: the visitor recognises a small independent label, reads three releases with one line each and their real pressing data, trusts the four promises, and opens a release.

FIRST VIEWPORT: masthead top-left (foil mark, "CyberTech team", one line), nav top-right; then full-width bands: giant catalogue number spanning about a third of the width at left, embossed icon, then name, one line, pressing row (version · date · macOS · licence), arrow at the far right; the newest band in orange with a "new release" sticker; the whole band is the link.

FORM: independent record-label catalogue, position 1 of my ordered seven (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, chosen by the user over the roll); seed: d897c646485cc574.
