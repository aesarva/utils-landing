# Lidless product page (/lidless/, /en/lidless/)

Scope: the Lidless product page in its own world, inside the shared shell. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Mac owners (ru/en) who leave long work running (builds, downloads, renders, AI agents, SSH, AirPlay) and walk away, arriving from GitHub, the hub or a shared link. Job: believe the Mac keeps working with the lid closed, trust that it is safe and that normal sleep always comes back, and download the signed DMG. Proof the user chose to lead with: closed-lid mode works. Further proof: the ON AIR sign as the one-press button with a countdown; safety rules (stops below 20% battery, pauses when hot, closed on battery up to 4 h, all tunable); recovery table (quit, crash, reboot: sleep comes back); real app renders in `src/assets/products/lidless/` (panel-timer, panel-indefinite, panel-off, panel-safety, panel-timer-ru, menubar states); release data from `releases.json` (v0.4.0, GPL-3.0, macOS 14+, universal). Facts from `../lidless` develop: README, README.ru, CHANGELOG. Constraints: no invented claims; red only for the ON AIR lamp/sign and on-air state; app UI is English and Russian (switchable); no JS required for content; `data-download` contract. Not wanted (user): template SaaS landing, Apple mimicry, hacker cyber style.

World: the broadcast studio wall at night, the app's own ON AIR sign built out to page scale.

Approved comp: pending (surface composition round).

Memorable moment: the glowing ON AIR sign is a real button on the page; press it and it lights and counts down, as in the app, over a closed laptop that keeps running.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Lidless's page is the studio wall when the red light is on: the lit ON AIR sign is the button and the proof, and the closed laptop under it is still on air. It refuses the centred icon, headline and feature-card grid of every keep-awake utility.

OWN-WORLD: charcoal acoustic-foam wall and dark studio wood, one red-glass ON AIR sign with off-white condensed lettering and a countdown, studio level meters for battery and heat with the stop line drawn, warm off-white text; red belongs to the lamp and on-air state only.

STORY: the visitor sees the sign lit over a closed laptop, reads that the work keeps running, sees the meters and the stop line that keep it safe, learns sleep always comes back, downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the studio wall; the ON AIR sign with countdown at hero scale; sessions 15 min, 1 h, 3 h, Until…, ∞ and closed-lid mode as studio controls; battery and heat meters; a closed laptop with its cable, captioned lid closed, still on air; name, line, one sentence and the download with version, size, macOS floor and licence.
