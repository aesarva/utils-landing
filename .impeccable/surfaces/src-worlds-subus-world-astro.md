# Subus product page (/subus/, /en/subus/)

Scope: the Subus product page in its own world, inside the shared shell. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Mac owners (ru/en) who pay for a dozen services and lose count, arriving from GitHub, the hub or a shared link. Job: believe nothing will be charged by surprise (the proof the user chose), see honest totals, trust it is local and private, and download the signed DMG. Proof: days until the next charge (the menu-bar counter), the upcoming timeline grouped Today & tomorrow / This week / This month / Later, reminders 5, 3 and 1 day before each charge and before a trial ends, totals per month and year at the Central Bank of Russia rate, real app renders in `src/assets/products/subus/` (upcoming light/dark en/ru, year window, spending, card, editor, status item), release data from `releases.json` (v1.0.0, GPL-3.0, macOS 14+). Facts from `../subus` develop README and README.ru. Constraints: demo subscriptions are labelled demo data and shown with monograms as the app does, never third-party logos; no invented claims; no JS required for content; `data-download` contract. Not wanted (user): template SaaS landing, Apple mimicry, fintech look, alarm.

World: a stack of cathode numeral tubes at night — the one number that matters, the days until the next charge, struck forward in glowing orange while its sister numerals hang as ghosts behind bronze gauze.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/subus/challenger-nixie.png` (the direction card comp; the user chose it as the composition).

Memorable moment: the "3" glows in its tube; the counter steps down as the visitor watches the next charge approach.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Subus's page is a numeral tube stack that answers one question before anything else: how many days until something is charged. It refuses the feature grid and the fintech dashboard of every subscription tracker.

OWN-WORLD: ink-blue black ground, cathode orange glow as the only hue (it is also the icon's dot), numerals with their unlit ghosts behind fine bronze gauze in glass tubes, bone-white grotesk prose, the real panel as the one piece of app UI.

STORY: the visitor sees the glowing 3 and reads it as days until the next charge, sees the amount and the monthly total in smaller tubes, scans the upcoming list, learns about the 5-3-1 reminders and that nothing leaves the Mac, downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: name and icon, the line and one sentence, the orange download with version, size, macOS floor and licence on the left; the tube stack centre with the large 3 (days until the next charge) and smaller tubes for the next amount and the monthly total; the real upcoming panel at right; the reminders section starting below.

FORM: cathode numeral stack (catalog operate-b-struck-cathode-gauze), a dealt challenger the user chose over the assigned coupon book (my position 7) and my pick (transit line, position 1); seed: seed:5ccdb9c8482fd652.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
